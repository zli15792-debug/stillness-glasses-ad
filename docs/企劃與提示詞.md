**共同調性**：森林、日式茶室、石桌、陶罐、茶。整體安靜、溫潤，帶有自然光的質感。
**目前缺少**：真人配戴鏡頭、手部互動、極近特寫（鉸鏈、鏡腳細節），以及收尾商品定格鏡頭。少了這些，會比較像「空間形象片」，購買的衝動比較難被激起。下面第三節的提示詞就是用來補這些鏡頭。

## 二、廣告概念：《靜觀》

> 一句話：**慢下來，才看得清。**

把眼鏡塑造成「讓你看見生活細節的物件」，而不只是一副眼鏡。

| 段落 | 情緒 | 鏡頭 | 字卡 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. 入靜（0–12 秒） | 好奇、放鬆 | 森林茶室全景 → 推軌 → 第一次瞥見眼鏡 | 有些風景，要慢下來才看得見 |
| 2. 相遇（12–28 秒） | 心動 | 白光轉場，主角眼鏡特寫、質感、焦點轉移 | 玳瑁紋理．溫潤如茶 |
| 3. 擁有（28–40 秒） | 想要 | 系列陳列、拉遠回到森林、收尾字卡 | 看見，自在 ／ SEE THE STILLNESS |

**配樂**：緩慢的氛圍鋼琴＋弦樂鋪底，在主角眼鏡出現時加入低頻「咚」的重音，最後漸弱收尾。
**特效**：2.39:1 電影寬銀幕黑邊、暖色調色、暗角、底片顆粒、緩慢推近、交叉溶接、白光閃轉場、淡入淡出黑場。

## 三、補拍提示詞（給 Google Flow 用）

> 寫成英文，Veo 對英文的理解比較穩定。每一段都沿用同一個場景描述，讓補拍的畫面和現有素材風格一致。

**共用風格句**（每段最後都加上）：
`Same scene: a Japanese tea room in a misty cedar forest, rough dark stone table, tatami, clay tea set, large dark ceramic jar. Tortoiseshell acetate glasses. Soft natural window light, muted warm tones, shallow depth of field, 35mm anamorphic film look, subtle grain, slow and calm camera movement. No text, no logos.`

### 1. 手拿起眼鏡（最重要，製造「擁有」的感覺）
```
Close-up: a woman's hand slowly picks up the tortoiseshell glasses from the stone table, light catches the lens edge, gentle rack focus from the stone texture to the frame. [共用風格句]
```

### 2. 真人配戴（讓觀眾想像自己戴上）
```
Medium close-up of a calm East Asian woman in her late 20s, linen clothing, sitting by the open window of the tea room, she puts on the tortoiseshell glasses and looks out at the forest, a soft smile, slow push-in. [共用風格句]
```

### 3. 鉸鏈與鏡腳微距
```
Extreme macro shot gliding along the temple arm of the tortoiseshell glasses to the metal hinge, the amber and brown pattern glowing in backlight, slow dolly. [共用風格句]
```

### 4. 鏡片裡的倒影
```
Macro shot through the lens of the tortoiseshell glasses resting on the stone table, the forest reflected in the lens, steam rising from a cup of tea in soft focus behind. [共用風格句]
```

### 5. 光影掃過鏡框
```
Static close-up of the tortoiseshell glasses on the stone table, a slow beam of sunlight moves across the frame as leaves sway outside, dust particles floating in the light. [共用風格句]
```

### 6. 收尾商品定格（放品牌名用）
```
Centered product shot of the tortoiseshell glasses standing upright on the stone table, front view, background softly blurred forest, very slow push-in, generous empty space above for a title. [共用風格句]
```
